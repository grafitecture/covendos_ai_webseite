Doctors have less time for visits. Patients need more support between appointments. And every new therapy has to prove itself within a few months.

Where the budget goes

Spending on communication with doctors in Germany, 2023

The face-to-face field force remains the core. But it is becoming hybrid, and it needs support that does not cost more headcount. That is exactly where we come in: agents prepare and follow up every contact. People lead it.

In Mannheim.
And across Germany

Our headquarters is in Mannheim, at the heart of the Rhine-Neckar region, one of Germany’s most important locations for healthcare service providers. From here we manage projects, quality and technology. The work itself happens everywhere: many of our pharma consultants, call agents and patient support coordinators work from home, from Kiel to Freiburg.

  • Mannheim
    Headquarters, remote detailing studio, academy and technology on Gottlieb-Daimler-Straße.
  • Working from home
    Inside pharma consultants, call agents and nurses work from their own region, connected by one platform and one contact history.
  • In the territory
    Field force and key account management wherever your centres, practices and pharmacies are.

For you, this means teams that are staffed quickly, regional proximity without a branch network, and workplaces that let us reach skilled people all over Germany.

    Lifecycle

    Every phase in the life of a medicine has a different problem. We put together the team and agents that the phase requires, and rebuild when the situation changes.

    1. 1

      Before launch

      The situation
      Target groups are vague, content is not yet approved, teams are not yet in place.
      Our contribution
      Segmentation, approvable content modules and a remote team in weeks rather than months.
    2. 2

      Launch and first year on the market

      The situation
      The benefit assessment is under way, and the negotiated reimbursement amount applies from the seventh month. Every contact with a doctor counts.
      Our contribution
      Hybrid launch teams, broad reach and medical information from day one.
    3. 3

      Growth

      The situation
      Prescribers know the product, but patients stop therapy too early.
      Our contribution
      Support programmes, adherence coaching and signals from patient care fed back to the brand team.
    4. 4

      Maturity

      The situation
      The field force is redeployed, and the brand slips down the portfolio.
      Our contribution
      Remote support for existing prescribers at a cost per contact well below field force level.
    5. 5

      Patent expiry and established brands

      The situation
      Generics, tenders, rebate contracts. Little budget, but loyal patients.
      Our contribution
      Lean, agent-supported management, up to taking over operational marketing.

    Agents

    Our agents assist. People decide.

    Every agent has a clearly defined task and a person who is responsible for it. It works only with approved content and hands over as soon as judgement or empathy is needed.

      Guardrails

      Designed for the HWG, the FSA Code, GDPR, the EU AI Act and the GVP guidelines on pharmacovigilance.

      • Every agent has a named person who is responsible for it.
      • Agents do not write new promotional claims. They combine approved modules.
      • Healthcare professionals and patients always know when they are talking to an AI.
      • Medical questions beyond first-line information are answered by people.
      • Data stays in the EU, and every interaction is logged traceably.

      In Mannheim and working from home across Germany

          Good to know
          The market in figures and rules
          
        
            
          
          • Pharma consultant status
            Anyone who gives doctors professional information about medicines needs the expertise required under Section 75 of the German Medicinal Products Act (AMG), explicitly also on the phone. You have it, for example, as a pharmacist, as a pharmaceutical, biological, chemical or medical technical assistant (PTA, BTA, CTA, MTA), with a degree in natural sciences or medicine, or as a certified pharma representative (IHK).
            • 
          
          • Rules of temporary staffing
            Only companies licensed by the Federal Employment Agency may provide temporary workers. A maximum of 18 months is permitted with the same client, and equal pay applies after nine months at the latest.
            • 
          
          • The labour market
            In June 2025, around 622,000 people in Germany worked in temporary agency jobs subject to social security contributions, 1.8 per cent of all employees. 61 per cent were back in employment 90 days after their assignment ended.
            • 
          
          • Salary
            For pharmaceutical sales representatives, salary portals put the 2026 averages at between around €50,500 and €66,000 gross per year. Experience, therapeutic area and region make the difference.
            • 
        
          
      
          
      
          Sources: German Medicinal Products Act (AMG) § 75; Temporary Employment Act (AÜG) §§ 1 and 8; Federal Employment Agency statistics, Entwicklungen in der Zeitarbeit (January 2026); jobvector salary analysis 2026; jobjump salary profile for pharma representatives 2026.
