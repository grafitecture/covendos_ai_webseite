          Healthklick and the model
          People lead, agents assist.
          
    
          
      
          What is Healthklick?
          Healthklick is a service of Grafitecture GmbH, Mannheim. We handle sales, patient services, medical information, omnichannel content and staffing for pharmaceutical companies. AI agents prepare, document and follow up. People lead every conversation that matters, and they make the decisions.
          
      
          Do AI agents replace the field force?
          No. In Germany, the face-to-face field force still takes the largest share of budgets for communication with doctors, around 70 per cent in 2023 1. Agents take routine work off its hands: briefings, documentation, compliance checks and follow-up. That leaves more time for the conversation itself.
          Where the field force cannot go, for example in vacant territories or for broad reach, eReps take over by video and phone.
          
      
          What does an agent at Healthklick do, and what does it never do?
          Every agent has a clearly defined task and a responsible person. It only works with approved content, never writes new promotional claims and does not answer medical questions beyond first-line information. As soon as judgement or empathy is needed, it hands over to a person.
          Healthcare professionals and patients always know when they are talking to an AI. Every interaction is logged in a traceable way.
          
      
          Which companies and markets does Healthklick work for?
          For manufacturers of prescription medicines (Rx) and of self-medication products (OTC). For Rx we only talk to healthcare professionals; for OTC also to pharmacies and consumers. Our focus is on launch and the first year on the market, specialty products with support programmes and established brands that need leaner support.
          
      
          How is Healthklick different from classic contract sales providers?
          Classic providers mainly supply headcount: field force, inside sales, call centres. Healthklick provides an operating model of people and agents with clearly assigned roles, one shared contact history and measurable conversation quality. Teams are ready faster, and services can be used in fractions of a full-time position.
          
      
          How does a collaboration start?
          With a pilot of around twelve weeks:
          • Weeks 1 to 3: choose a pilot territory, measure the baseline, approve content for the agents.
          • Weeks 4 to 8: briefing and documentation agents in tandem with two or three team members.
          • Weeks 9 to 12: evaluation and decision on the roll-out.
          
    
          
  
          
  
          
    
          Market and channels
          How healthcare professionals are reached today.
          
    
          
      
          How do pharmaceutical companies reach doctors today?
          More selectively than before. In the five largest European markets, only just over half of doctors were accessible to the field force in 2024 (53 per cent), and 62 per cent of those accessible met with three companies or fewer 2. Whoever has access needs to prepare every contact well and follow up in a coordinated way across channels.
          
      
          Is remote detailing worth it when its share of the budget is so small?
          Remote channels accounted for four to five per cent of spending on communication with doctors in 2023 1. That says little about their value: video and phone conversations can be planned, reach practices the field force rarely visits and are well suited to quick follow-up.
          The tandem is strongest: the field force for centres and top prescribers, eReps for broad reach, agents for preparation and follow-up, all with the same contact history.
          
      
          Don’t CRM vendors already offer their own AI agents?
          Yes. In December 2025, Veeva made AI agents available for Vault CRM, including agents for call preparation and voice capture 3. But the tool alone does not change the work. What matters is the operating model behind it: who does which task, which content is approved and who is accountable for the result. Wherever possible, we build on the systems your team already uses.
          
      
          How is the pharmacy market changing?
          The number of pharmacies is falling: there were 16,601 at the end of 2025, and in September 2026 the figure dropped below 16,500 for the first time 4. At the same time, e-prescriptions have strengthened mail order; its share of the Rx business doubled to around two per cent in 2025 5.
          For OTC brands this means fewer but more important pharmacies, and teams that value good training and short lines to the manufacturer. That is exactly where telesales and remote training come in.
          
    
          
  
          
  
          
    
          Lifecycle and market access
          Every phase has a different problem.
          
    
          
      
          Why are the first months after launch so decisive?
          Because in Germany the negotiated reimbursement price already applies from the seventh month after launch 6. Awareness, first prescriptions and experience in care have to be built within a few months. Hybrid launch teams, medical information from day one and a support programme that is ready before the first patients shorten this ramp-up.
          
      
          What is changing in AMNOG right now?
          Since the Medical Research Act (Medizinforschungsgesetz), manufacturers can agree a confidential reimbursement price under certain conditions 6. In parallel, the European joint clinical assessment (EU HTA) is being phased in: since 2025 for cancer medicines and advanced therapies, from 2028 for orphan drugs and from 2030 for further medicines 7.
          In the pharma dialogue, the Federal Ministry of Health is discussing further changes, such as a new benefit assessment three years after market entry. Nothing has been decided yet (as of November 2026) 8.
          
      
          What happens to a brand after patent expiry?
          Generics, tenders and rebate contracts push down price and budget, while many patients stay loyal to their therapy. This phase does not need large field teams but lean, reliable support for existing prescribers and pharmacies. Agents handle reminders, documentation and market signals; people lead the conversations.
          
    
          
  
          
  
          
    
          Law and compliance
          Governance is part of the design.
          
    
          
      
          Are AI agents allowed to talk to doctors?
          Anyone who informs healthcare professionals about medicines needs the expertise of a pharmaceutical representative under section 75 of the German Medicines Act (AMG), explicitly also by phone 9. That is why professional conversations at Healthklick are led by people with this qualification. Agents assist, for example with briefings, appointment suggestions or approved information on request.
          Since 2 August 2026, the EU AI Act has also required that people can recognise when they are interacting with an AI system (Article 50) 10.
          
      
          Is an agent in pharma sales a high-risk system under the AI Act?
          As a rule, no: sales, marketing and medical information are not among the high-risk areas in Annex III. Every use case still has to be checked, for example if AI is to contribute to staffing decisions, because employment is expressly listed there.
          The Digital Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) postponed the obligations for high-risk systems to December 2027; the transparency obligations under Article 50 have applied since August 2026 10.
          
      
          What can agents say about a medicine?
          The same as any other promotional material: only what the German Medicinal Products Advertising Act (HWG) allows. Prescription medicines may only be advertised to doctors, dentists, vets, pharmacists and authorised traders (section 10 HWG) 11. That is why our agents do not write their own claims. They combine modules that medical, regulatory and legal have approved once, including the mandatory information.
          
      
          How are possible side effects captured?
          Every interaction, whether a conversation, email or chat, is checked for indications of side effects, medication errors or use during pregnancy. A hit creates an initial report, which a specialist reviews within one working day and passes on to your pharmacovigilance team.
          The marketing authorisation holder must report serious suspected cases to EudraVigilance within 15 days and non-serious ones within 90 days 12. Every day counts.
          
      
          How does Healthklick handle health data?
          Health data enjoys special protection under Article 9 GDPR. In patient programmes we only process it with explicit consent, on behalf of the pharmaceutical company and on the basis of a data processing agreement. Data stays in the EU, every interaction is logged in a traceable way, and data is only stored for as long as necessary.
          
      
          May doctors be contacted by email?
          Under section 7 of the German Unfair Competition Act (UWG), advertising by email requires prior express consent, including in business dealings 13. That is why we work with “email on request”: the doctor asks for information in a conversation or online, the consent is documented, and only then is approved content sent.
          
      
          What role does the FSA Code play?
          The FSA Code for Healthcare Professionals of the German voluntary self-regulation body for the pharmaceutical industry governs how member companies work with doctors, pharmacists and other healthcare professionals, for example regarding training events, fees and benefits. In 2025, the FSA had 57 members 14. When we work for a member company, its code obligations also apply to our team and our agents.
          
    
          
  
          
  
          
    
          Patient services
          Time for the questions between two appointments.
          
    
          
      
          May pharmaceutical companies support patients directly?
          Yes, if the programme supports the therapy and does not promote a medicine. For prescription products, the ban on advertising to the public applies (section 10 HWG), and inadmissible benefits are prohibited (section 7 HWG) 1115. Support programmes are therefore reviewed for advertising, data protection and professional law before they start. Medical decisions always remain with the doctor.
          
      
          Why do practices so rarely recommend support programmes?
          In conversations with practices we keep hearing the same reasons: it is unclear what the programme does, its benefit for the practice remains invisible, and nobody thinks of it when a new patient starts therapy. A good programme says in one sentence what it does, gives patients time for everyday questions and, with consent, informs the practice about the enrolment, for example via a QR code in two minutes.
          
      
          Are reports from patient programmes pharmacovigilance cases?
          Yes. Indications of side effects from organised programmes such as patient support count as solicited reports under GVP Module VI and must be captured, assessed and reported 12. At Healthklick the reporting process is therefore tested for all channels before the first patient is enrolled.

A maximum of 18 consecutive months with the same client is allowed, and the same pay as the permanent staff applies after nine months at the latest 16. Healthklick supports the selection and the assignment.
          
      
          What qualification do I need as a pharmaceutical representative?
          The expertise required under section 75 AMG, for example as a pharmacist, as a PTA, BTA, CTA or MTA, with a degree in natural sciences or medicine, or as a certified pharmaceutical representative (IHK) 9. This also applies to information given by phone. For roles in patient support, telesales or customer service there are other routes, for example with nursing training.
          
      
          Can I apply without pharma experience?
          Yes. Many routes start with onboarding at the Healthklick Academy, including after a career break or as a career changer. You do not need a cover letter: a few details and your CV are enough.
          All roles require fluent German.
